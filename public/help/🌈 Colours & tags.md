# 🌈 Colours & tags

Open the side panel (`S`) with a card selected:

- **Colour:** pick a swatch. Children **inherit** the colour by default, so a whole branch shares a hue.
- **Inherit** (the first swatch) is the default; **none** stops inheriting.
- **Any colour:** the spectrum swatch opens your device's own colour picker, so you're not limited to
  the palette. The card's text flips between light and dark on its own to stay readable on whatever
  you pick — pale colours get dark text, deep ones get light text.
- **Your recent colours** sit next to the spectrum swatch, newest first, so a colour you picked once
  is one tap away for the next card. The map's own custom colours show up there too, which is what
  you see after opening it on another device.
- Card colours are the **same in light and dark theme**, so a map looks like itself either way.
- **Tags** are plain frontmatter — add your own for filtering and notes.
