# 🌗 Edges & theme

- **Draw a line:** select a card, then drag from one of the four rings on its edges and drop anywhere on another card. With two or more cards selected, `⌘ L` connects them in order.
- A line runs straight from card to card and follows them when they move.
- **Click a line** to change its colour, make it dashed, pick its ends (arrow, dot or none) or delete it. **Double-click** it to give it a label. Drag one of its end handles onto another card to move that end.
- **Theme:** toggle **light / dark** in the toolbar; it's saved for next time.
- Both are visual only — they never change your notes.
