# 📝 Edit text

- **Double-click opens a card for editing.** Where you hit it decides what opens: the **title** starts
  a rename, anywhere **else** on the card opens its note. On a touch screen, double-tap.

- **Rename:** double-click the title, press `F2`, or use the rename button.

- **Edit the note:** double-click a card's body, or press `E`, to type the note right on the card. Press `↓` while renaming to drop into the body.

- **Double-click empty space** on the canvas to make a new card right there, ready to name. A stack
  takes a new row at the end. On a **frame** the gesture means "go in" instead — and once you're
  inside one, double-clicking the canvas comes back out. See "Open a frame".

- **Reorder a note without opening it.** Select one card and a small **grip** appears beside every
  part of its note — each paragraph, each heading, and **each item of a list**. Click a grip to
  select that piece (it lights up), then drag it up or down to move it, or press `⌥/Alt ↑` and
  `⌥/Alt ↓`. `Esc` lets go of the piece; press it again to deselect the card.

- **Drag a grip off the card** and that piece leaves the note: drop it on empty canvas and it becomes
  a card of its own, drop it on another card and it joins that note.

- `Enter` adds a line · `Esc` cancels · click away to keep. **It autosaves.**
