- **Fold a branch:** click the round button at the card's top-right corner. It appears when you
  hover a card, or when the card is selected — handy on a touch screen: tap the card, then the button.
  Frames and stacks have one too, on the corner of their title, and with several cards selected each
  shows its own — clicking any of them folds the whole selection.
- On a **tab group**, the button sits on the open tab and folds the whole group away to a single pill.
  Click its `+N` to bring the box back with that same tab open.
- A folded card shows that same button as `+5` — the number is how many cards are hidden underneath.
  Click it to open the branch again.
- Pressing `X` does the same thing, and folds every selected card at once.
  (Double-click no longer folds — it opens a card for editing instead. See "Edit text".)
- With a card selected, `←` folds its branch and `→` opens it — see the shortcuts page for walking
  the tree with the arrow keys.
- A card with a note but no children can fold too: it tucks the note away and shows just the title.
- Collapsing keeps big maps readable — open only the branch you're working in.
