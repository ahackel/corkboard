# 🖼️ Paste images

- **Paste** an image from the clipboard (`Ctrl` / `⌘` + `V`) onto a card, or **drop** an image file on the canvas.
- It's saved alongside your notes and shown inline in the card body.
- PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP and **SVG** all work the same way; a new card takes the picture's own shape.
- A card that holds **nothing but one image** *is* that image: no padding, and it resizes as a whole,
  keeping its proportions. Shrink it right down and it becomes a small square icon; scale that back up,
  or use the corner button, to open it out again.
- Press `Space` on a selected image to view it full screen — `‹` / `›` (or `←` / `→`) walk through the
  other pictures alongside it, and `Space` again closes.
- You can also paste plain text straight onto a card to fill its note.
